I'm Crystal. I make things, chase new interests, and collect them with very little concern for how many are already in progress.

I'm interested in technology, food, language, and the hidden processes behind things. I have a habit of getting curious about something and following it considerably further than I originally intended.

My professional background includes applied linguistics, data science, customer experience, and QA. Cooking was a past life.

I'm surrounded by exercise equipment and currently fixated on ballet and yoga.

This site is somewhere to put my [[notes/index|Notes]], [[projects/index|Projects]], and [[recipes/index|Recipes]] without pretending they're finished or particularly related. Some of it is about learning with technology. Some of it is experimentation. Some of it is simply something I found interesting enough to write down.
